Where I come from

Car dealerships were my Disneyland.

I have been obsessed with cars since I was a little kid. I played in my parents’ cars in the driveway. When I was old enough to have a camera, all I photographed was cars. And when it was time for our family to get a new car, the best weekend of the year was going from dealership to dealership with my parents. Other kids wanted Disneyland. I wanted the showroom.

In 2000 I turned that obsession into a career and spent fifteen years inside more than a dozen Los Angeles dealerships, from Santa Monica Volkswagen to Beverly Hills Porsche, with Honda and Audi along the way. That is where you learn how cars are really sourced and priced, how a deal gets structured, where the add-ons hide, and what separates a genuinely good deal from one that only looks good on the window sticker.

My customers ranged from working-class families to A-list celebrities, and the job was the same for both: listen, be straight, and find the car that fits the life, not the one that is convenient to sell.

In 2014 my family and I moved from LA to San Luis Obispo. The next year I bought Certified Auto Repair, which I have run ever since. Sales taught me how a deal works. The shop taught me what a car is really like after the sale.

Fifteen years selling cars and more than ten years fixing them is a rare combination. It is what makes me useful to you, whether you are buying, selling, or just trying to figure out what to do next.

The shop

A lift, a mechanic, and a straight answer.

I co-own Certified Auto Repair in downtown San Luis Obispo. When I check a used car for you, it goes on a lift there and gets a full inspection before you spend a dollar. A history report tells you what was reported; the lift tells you what is actually there.

If I recommend my own shop, I’ll say so. You’re welcome to choose another shop; we’ll agree on its cost before booking.

How I work

Cars are personal. So is the advice.

The school run, the commute, the things you carry, the roads you look forward to driving. Those details matter as much as the badge on the hood, so I start by asking about them.

You hire me, so I work for you and nobody else. You pay a flat fee we agree on up front, and no dealer pays me anything. If the car is wrong for you, I’ll tell you. If the deal can be better, I’ll push for it, politely and with numbers.

We agree on the scope, the fee and the timing before any work starts, and when you reach out, you reach me, not a call center.

San Luis Obispo has been home since 2014, so the people I help are neighbors I will see again. That is how I like it.
