Questions and answers

What does 805CarGuy do?

I help people in and around San Luis Obispo buy and sell cars with less stress. Buying used, I check the car and the deal before you commit. Buying new, I help you compare models and dealer quotes. Selling, I handle as much of the listing and the sale as you want. I’m Isaac, and you work directly with me.

How much does it cost to have a used car checked in San Luis Obispo?

$249 total for one car in the city of San Luis Obispo. That includes picking the car up from the dealership, a test drive, transport to Certified Auto Repair, a full used-car inspection and a written deal analysis. Payment is due when booking. A car elsewhere in SLO County or beyond needs a separate quote.

Do I have to live in San Luis Obispo?

No. You can live anywhere. For the $249 price the car needs to be in the city of San Luis Obispo so I can pick it up, drive it and get it to the shop. That works well if you’re buying a car here from out of town.

Is a vehicle history report enough?

No. A history report only shows what was reported to it. It can’t tell you about the brakes, the tires, a leak, or an accident nobody reported. The Federal Trade Commission recommends an independent mechanical inspection before you buy a used car, and that is exactly what the $249 assessment includes.

No. I work for you, not for a dealership. I co-own Certified Auto Repair in San Luis Obispo, which is where the inspections happen, and I’ll always say so. If you’d rather use another shop, we agree on its cost first.

I read it myself and reply the way you asked for, by email, text or a call. We talk through what you need and agree on the scope, the fee and when payment is due before any work starts. Sending an inquiry doesn’t book anything or cost anything.
