A few useful answers

Before we talk.

Do I have to book or pay to get in touch?

No. Tell me what you need and how you prefer to hear back. We’ll discuss the right help, timing, and price before any work begins.

What if I have no idea which car I want?

That’s a good place to start. The choosing consultation includes a 45–60 minute conversation, three recommendations with suggested years and trims, and one follow-up after you’ve considered them or taken test drives. The first five consultation clients pay $49.

What’s different about buying new or used?

With a used car, the individual vehicle’s condition, records, and history matter. With a new car, the work usually centers on configuration, availability, the written deal, and the buying experience. Hard-to-find specifications may need a separately agreed search.

What does the $1,000 local used-car service include?

Choosing, searching, screening, negotiation, purchase coordination, and one full used-car inspection at Certified Auto Repair in San Luis Obispo. Payment is $500 to start and $500 when you purchase, less applicable prior payments. The car must be available for inspection at the shop, subject to seller permission and scheduling. Transport is not included.

If you reject that car after inspection, the final $500 is not due unless you complete a purchase. Any additional inspections are priced and approved before booking.

How many cars does a used-car search cover?

At least three qualifying, seller-confirmed candidates within the agreed 30-day search, unless you choose a car sooner. The $500 search includes help choosing a model if needed, seller contact, review of available history and maintenance records, and a recommendation explaining which cars are worth pursuing. We agree on the budget and must-haves first. If I can’t deliver the agreed search, we discuss an extension or refund. I won’t fill the shortlist with unsuitable cars to make up the count.

Is a remote evaluation an inspection?

No. The $99 evaluation reviews the listing or written offer and the records available. It helps you decide what to pursue or question, but it cannot establish mechanical condition. Certified Auto Repair charges $199 for its full used-car inspection; one is included in the complete $1,000 local used-car service.

Does my car have to be local?

Advice and written-deal reviews can happen remotely. The $1,000 complete used-car package includes an inspection at Certified Auto Repair in San Luis Obispo. If the car can’t come to the shop, we’ll agree on the scope and outside inspection costs first.

Where does my car stay while you help sell it?

With you by default. Drop-off is by arrangement when space is available. Detailing, repairs, smog, paid advertising, and transport are separately approved costs.
