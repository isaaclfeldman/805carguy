Frequently asked

Common questions.

Do you work with new vehicles, or only used?

Both. New, used, certified pre-owned, private-party sales, and out-of-state purchases. If there is a vehicle and a deal to be made, I can help you navigate it.

How does Service Coordination differ from a regular shop or dealer?

I do not perform the work. I help you evaluate quotes, identify whether a recommended repair is necessary, route you to trustworthy local shops, and stay on top of warranty and recall matters. It is a layer of advocacy between you and the service writer — without the markup.

I am not ready to buy yet. Can you still help?

Yes — that is often the best time to engage. A short message exchange can help you clarify what you actually need, what timing makes sense, and whether the market is in your favor before you commit to anything.

How does payment work?

After scope is agreed, I send a secure payment link. Once that is settled, we set a plan and begin. No hidden fees, no upsells, no surprises.

What area do you cover?

San Luis Obispo County and surrounding communities — San Luis Obispo, Paso Robles, Atascadero, Santa Maria, Arroyo Grande, Pismo Beach, Morro Bay, Nipomo, Templeton, and Los Osos. Message me your zip code if you are uncertain.

Are you affiliated with any dealership?

No. The service is fully independent. No dealer pays me, refers business in exchange for kickbacks, or has any influence over the recommendations I make.
