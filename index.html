A few useful answers

Before we talk.

Do I have to book or pay to get in touch?

No. Tell me what you need and how you prefer to hear back. Before work begins, we put the scope and price in writing. For one purchase or sale, that includes the candidates or deal revisions covered, timeframe, outside costs, cancellation or refund terms, and when each payment is due.

What if I have no idea which car I want?

That’s a good place to start. The choosing consultation includes a 45–60 minute conversation, at least three tailored recommendations with suggested years and trims, and one follow-up after you’ve considered them or taken test drives. The introductory price is $49.

What’s different about buying new or used?

With a used car, the individual vehicle’s condition, records, and history matter. With a new car, the work usually centers on configuration, availability, the written deal, and the buying experience. Hard-to-find specifications may need a separately agreed search.

Can you help with a new car and the dealership process?

Yes. I can help compare the car and trim, written dealer offers, add-ons, trade-in figures, and financing or lease terms. You can start with a written second opinion or get help through the purchase. We agree on the work first; dealer requirements and any in-person steps still need to be confirmed. See how new-car buying help works.

Can you check a local car if I live somewhere else?

Tell me where the car is and what you need checked. We can discuss a seller visit, shop inspection, or pickup by arrangement as part of a complete car-and-deal evaluation. Scope, timing, access, and one total quote are agreed first. A remote review is not required before asking about local help. Explore the local assessment.

How do search, purchase help, and complete service differ?

$500 used-car search: I find and screen actual cars, contact sellers, and explain which candidates are worth pursuing. You handle negotiation and the purchase.

$750 purchase assistance: I help with new-car offers or a used car you’ve found, including negotiation, deal review, and purchase coordination. A used-car search and physical inspection are separate.

$1,000 complete local used-car service: Choosing, search, screening, negotiation, and purchase coordination, with one full inspection at Certified Auto Repair included.

What does the $1,000 local used-car service include?

Choosing, searching, screening, negotiation, purchase coordination, and one full used-car inspection at Certified Auto Repair in San Luis Obispo. That inspection is included in the $1,000 price. Payment is $500 to start and $500 when you purchase, less applicable prior payments. Our written agreement confirms the purchase covered and when its balance is due.

The car must be available for inspection at the shop, subject to seller permission and scheduling. Transport is not included. If you reject that car after inspection, the final $500 is not due unless you complete a purchase. Any additional inspections are priced and approved before booking.

What do I get with the $500 used-car search?

At least three qualifying, seller-confirmed candidates within the agreed 30-day search, unless you choose a car sooner. I help choose a model if needed, contact sellers to confirm availability, specifications, and price, and review available history and maintenance records. You get a recommendation explaining which cars are worth pursuing and why.

We agree on your budget and must-haves first. Physical inspection and purchase negotiation are separate. If I can’t deliver the agreed search, we discuss an extension or refund under our written agreement. I won’t fill the shortlist with unsuitable cars to make up the count.

How does an upgrade credit work?

Money you’ve already paid counts once toward an applicable larger service for the same purchase or sale. For example, if you’ve paid $500 for a used-car search and upgrade to the $1,000 complete local service, $500 remains. Our written agreement confirms the credit and remaining payments before the added work starts.

Is a remote evaluation an inspection?

No. The $99 evaluation reviews one used-car listing and available records, or one written new-car offer. It helps you decide what to pursue or question, but it cannot establish mechanical condition.

Certified Auto Repair’s standalone full used-car inspection costs $199. That is the shop’s price, not an 805CarGuy service fee. One inspection is already included in the $1,000 complete local service, with no extra $199 charge.

Does my car have to be local?

Advice and written-deal reviews can happen remotely. The $1,000 complete used-car package includes an inspection at Certified Auto Repair in San Luis Obispo. If the car can’t come to the shop, we’ll agree on the scope and outside inspection costs first.

Which selling service makes sense for my car?

The $350 assisted sale covers pricing guidance, photos, a listing, and initial buyer screening for one car over 30 days. You handle showings, negotiations, and closing.

Full service also handles showings and negotiation and coordinates closing over 60 days. The fee is 5% of the sale price, with a $1,000 minimum: a $10,000 or $20,000 sale costs $1,000; a $30,000 sale costs $1,500. The $350 upfront payment counts toward that total. It covers completed preparation even if the car doesn’t sell.

For a lower-value car, the $350 assisted service may make more sense if you can handle the buyer meetings and sale yourself. Detailing, repairs, smog, paid advertising, and transport are separate costs agreed in advance.

Where does my car stay while you help sell it?

With you by default. Drop-off is by arrangement when space is available. Detailing, repairs, smog, paid advertising, and transport are separately approved costs.
