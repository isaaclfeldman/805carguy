One car in SLO city

Car and deal assessment · $249 total

Payment is due when booking. For one car in the city of San Luis Obispo, this includes:

  • Dealership pickup, a test drive, and transport to Certified Auto Repair.
  • One full used-car inspection at the shop.
  • Isaac’s written deal analysis, connecting the available records, inspection findings and price to your decision.

Ask about a car assessment ↗

Complete local used-car help · $1,000 total

$500 to start and $500 when you purchase, less applicable prior payments credited once. This covers one purchase.

  • Choosing, an agreed 30-day search, screening, negotiation and purchase coordination.
  • At least three qualifying, seller-confirmed cars unless you choose one sooner, plus available-record review and a recommendation.
  • One full inspection at Certified Auto Repair in San Luis Obispo, included in the $1,000 total.

The car must come to the SLO shop. Transport is not included in this package. Additional inspections are separately priced and approved. Any agreed personal visit or test drive depends on seller permission, access, travel and scheduling.

Rejecting a car after inspection does not by itself trigger the final payment. If I cannot deliver the agreed search, we agree on an extension or refund under our written agreement.

Ask about a full-service sale ↗

Full-service examples: a $10,000 or $20,000 sale has a $1,000 total fee; a $30,000 sale has a $1,500 fee. The $350 upfront is part of that total. Assisted selling may fit a lower-value car when you can handle buyer meetings and closing.

Detailing, repairs, smog, paid advertising and transport are separately approved costs. We agree on photo and showing arrangements and responsibilities before starting. A sale is not guaranteed.
