Help me sell.

Get the listing and initial buyer screening handled, or get help managing the sale. The right scope depends on the car and how much you want to handle yourself.

Assisted sale · $350

One car, 30 days, paid upfront. I help with pricing, photos, a listing and initial buyer screening. You handle showings, negotiation and closing. The car stays with you.

Ask about an assisted sale ↗
Full-service sale · 5%, $1,000 minimum

One car over 60 days, with pricing, photos, listing preparation, buyer communication, showings, negotiation and closing coordination. The car stays with you by default; drop-off is by arrangement when space is available.

$350 upfront counts toward the total, with the balance due on sale. That upfront payment covers completed preparation even if the car does not sell.

A $20,000 sale has a $1,000 total fee: $350 upfront and $650 on sale. The minimum also means a $10,000 sale has a $1,000 fee. Assisted selling may make more sense for a lower-value car if you can handle the showings and closing.

Ask about a full-service sale ↗

Detailing, repairs, smog, paid advertising and transport are separately approved costs. We agree on where photos and showings happen, which listings and buyer messages I handle, and the closing responsibilities before starting. A sale is not guaranteed.
