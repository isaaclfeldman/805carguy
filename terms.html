Before we get started

Service information.

An inquiry starts a conversation with Isaac about the car and the help you need.

Agree on the work first.

The homepage and service guide describe the available help and prices. Sending an inquiry does not book a service or take a payment.

Before work begins, we confirm the scope and price in a written agreement for one purchase or sale. It covers the candidates, offers, or deal revisions included, the timeframe, any outside costs, cancellation or refund terms, and when each payment is due. You know what is included before you commit.

Choose the help that fits.

You can ask for help choosing or searching for a car, buying, selling, or reviewing a listing or written offer. You make the final decision about a purchase or sale.

Payments already made count once toward an applicable larger service for the same purchase or sale. For example, $500 already paid for a used-car search leaves $500 toward the $1,000 complete local service. The written agreement confirms the credit and remaining payments before added work begins.

Search and purchase help.

The $500 used-car search covers an agreed 30 days and at least three qualifying, seller-confirmed candidates, unless you choose a car sooner. It includes available-record review and a recommendation. You handle the purchase; negotiation and a physical inspection are separate. If Isaac cannot deliver the agreed search, you agree on an extension or refund under the written agreement.

The $750 purchase assistance covers new-car offers or a used car you have found, including negotiation, deal review, and purchase coordination. A used-car search and physical inspection are separate.

The $1,000 complete local used-car service adds choosing, the agreed 30-day search, and one shop inspection. Payment is $500 to start and $500 when you purchase, less applicable prior payments. The written agreement identifies the purchase covered and when its balance is due. Rejecting a car after inspection does not by itself trigger the final payment.

Inspections and outside costs.

A listing or records review is not a physical inspection. Certified Auto Repair in San Luis Obispo, which Isaac co-owns, charges $199 for its standalone full used-car inspection. This is the shop’s price, not an 805CarGuy service fee.

One full inspection at that shop is already included in the $1,000 complete local used-car service. There is no extra $199 charge for that included inspection. The car must come to the shop with seller permission and scheduling.

Transport, additional inspections, repairs, detailing, smog, and paid advertising are separate costs to agree on before proceeding. Work at another shop or outside the local area needs its own agreed scope and costs.

Selling help.

The $350 assisted sale covers one car for 30 days, paid upfront. Isaac helps with pricing, photos, the listing, and initial buyer screening. You handle showings, negotiation, and closing. A sale is not guaranteed.

Full service covers one car for 60 days and also handles showings and negotiation and coordinates closing. The fee is 5% of the sale price, with a $1,000 minimum. A $10,000 or $20,000 sale costs $1,000; a $30,000 sale costs $1,500. The $350 upfront payment counts once toward the total, with the balance due on sale. It covers completed preparation even if the car does not sell.

For a lower-value car, the $350 assisted sale may make more sense if you can handle the buyer meetings and sale yourself. The car stays with you by default; drop-off is by arrangement when space is available. Outside costs are agreed separately.
