Before we get started

Service information.

An inquiry starts a conversation with Isaac about the car and the help you need.

Agree on the work first.

The homepage and inquiry guide describe the available services and starting prices. Before work begins, we confirm the scope, price, timing, and any outside costs. Sending an inquiry does not book a service or take a payment.

Choose the help that fits.

You can ask for help choosing or searching for a car, buying, selling, or reviewing a listing or written offer. You make the final decision about a purchase or sale. Payments already made count once toward an applicable larger service for the same purchase or sale.

Inspections and outside costs.

A listing or records review is not a physical inspection. The complete local used-car purchase service includes one full inspection at Certified Auto Repair in San Luis Obispo, which Isaac co-owns. The car must come to the shop with seller permission and scheduling.

Transport, additional inspections, repairs, detailing, smog, and paid advertising are separate costs to agree on before proceeding. Work at another shop or outside the local area needs its own agreed scope and costs.

Tell me what you need.

Include your timing and any questions about the service. Isaac will confirm availability and the next step before you commit to work.
