I can go where the car is.

Depending on the vehicle and seller, the agreed service can include a seller visit, road test and inspection at Certified Auto Repair. In many cases, I can collect the car and drive it to the shop, evaluating how it drives along the way. Pickup is by arrangement.

We confirm access, seller permission, the checks to be performed, timing, and any pickup and return plan before booking. A visit at the seller’s location has different access and equipment from an inspection at the shop; your quote and report will identify the work included.

The service brings the local work and written buying assessment together in one agreed scope. Finding other cars, negotiating for you and completing the purchase are available through purchase help and are included only if we expressly agree on them.

Report outline

What your report covers.

Your report connects the evidence about this car to the decision in front of you. These are the report’s sections; completed findings depend on the agreed checks and the information available.

The car and the evidence

The vehicle and VIN, the listing and records reviewed, and the date and scope of the assessment. Seller statements, supplied documents and my observations are identified separately. Missing records or conflicting details are called out.

Condition and driving findings

Findings from the agreed visit, road test and shop inspection, with supporting photos where available. Needed work, available repair estimates and items requiring further diagnosis are distinguished. The report states what could not be checked.

The price and the written deal

How the asking price compares with relevant available listings, accounting for condition, mileage and needed work. I review the supplied written offer for its vehicle price, fees, add-ons and conditions. Asking prices and repair estimates are labeled as estimates or advertised amounts.

A buying recommendation

A clear explanation of whether I would pursue the car, seek answers or a revised deal, or move on—and why. You’ll see the material concerns, the remaining unknowns and what I would want resolved before proceeding. We go through the assessment together.

An assessment reflects the evidence available at the time. Some faults may not appear during a visit or road test, and a seller may not provide every record or a complete written offer. Those limits stay visible in the report; I won’t fill them in with assumptions.

Send the car and the question you need answered.

Start with the listing link, the vehicle’s location, your timing and your main concerns. If you have the VIN, a written offer, maintenance records or a history report, mention what’s available. Tell me whether you’re considering a seller visit, a shop appointment or pickup to discuss.

I’ll confirm feasibility and the complete scope and price. The agreed work, follow-up and any outside costs are clear before you commit. Sending an inquiry does not book the service or authorize contact with the seller.
